Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Cambinos Collections LLC respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit our website, participate in the Cambinos private beta, or communicate with us.

1. Information You Provide

We may receive personal information that you voluntarily provide when you join our waitlist, contact Cambinos Collections LLC by email, or use another communication channel.

This information may include your name, email address, collecting interests, referral information, message content, and any other information you choose to provide.

2. Website and Technical Information

Our website hosting and infrastructure providers may automatically process limited technical information when you visit the website.

This may include your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, approximate location, and information about how the website is accessed.

This information may be used for security, website performance, reliability, and basic traffic measurement.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and support requests.
  • Operate, maintain, and secure our website.
  • Improve Cambinos products and services.
  • Manage the Cambinos waitlist and private beta invitations.
  • Communicate important company or product information.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • Process official-store orders, payments, delivery, returns, and customer support.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Official Store and Payments

If you make a purchase, Cambinos and its payment and shipping providers may process your name, contact information, order details, payment confirmation, delivery address, tracking information, and support history. Cambinos does not intentionally store full payment-card numbers.

This information is used to complete and protect the transaction, deliver the order, prevent fraud, provide support, and meet accounting and legal obligations.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The current Cambinos Collections website does not intentionally use advertising cookies.

Our hosting, security, or infrastructure providers may use essential technologies needed to deliver and protect the website.

6. How Information May Be Shared

Cambinos Collections LLC does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers that help us operate our website, communications, security, and future services. These providers may process information only as needed to perform services for Cambinos Collections LLC.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, property, or security of Cambinos Collections LLC, our users, or others.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to help protect information. However, no website, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, respond to communications, operate the business, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Cambinos Collections website is not intended to collect personal information directly from children under 13.

A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact us to request its review or deletion.

10. Cambinos Private Beta

The Cambinos private beta includes account, collection-management, scanning, catalog, pricing, binder, showcase, and test Marketplace features. The beta may receive your account email, profile choices, collection records, item details, scan photos and videos, device and diagnostic context, preferences, and feedback you choose to submit.

When you explicitly consent before using the Scanner, collectible images you choose may be securely uploaded and processed by automated and artificial-intelligence-assisted tools and service providers to identify items, compare catalog evidence, assess visible details, and improve reliability. Do not include faces, addresses, identification documents, or unrelated private information in scan media.

Scan media is private to your verified account unless you deliberately use it in a Marketplace listing or another sharing feature. Beta feedback is visible to the Cambinos beta team and may include the app screen, platform, and version needed to investigate the report. You can export collection data, control public Showcase visibility, and request account deletion from Settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Cambinos Collections develops. The updated date displayed at the top of this page will identify the latest version.
